# Psychology: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Psychologists** — stable career ID `psychologists`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `19-3030`.

BLS umbrella for clinical/counseling, school, I-O and all other psychology; neuropsychology examples cover only residual subtypes.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 19-3032.00 Industrial-Organizational Psychologists; 19-3033.00 Clinical and Counseling Psychologists; 19-3034.00 School Psychologists; 19-3039.00 Psychologists, All Other; 19-3039.02 Neuropsychologists; 19-3039.03 Clinical Neuropsychologists.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 209,400 jobs in 2025; +5.6% projected change, 2025–35; 11,300 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 19-3030](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

APA practice guidance retains professional judgment and identifies privacy, misinformation, and liability constraints; its practitioner survey describes self-reported clinical use. Screening assistance and notes can be useful, but assessment, therapeutic relationships and crisis decisions differ sharply from research or organizational practice. Digital therapy products are not automatically equivalent to licensed care.

**Independent sources:** [Ethical guidance for AI in professional practice of health service psychology](https://www.apa.org/topics/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/ethical-guidance-ai-professional-practice.html), [2025 Practitioner Pulse Survey: AI in therapist's office](https://www.apa.org/pubs/reports/practitioner/2025), [The AI Jobs Transition Framework](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/the-ai-jobs-transition-framework_report.pdf).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Validated documentation support may increase clinical capacity; low-risk psychoeducation may be more widely available. Organizational analytics and academic research may automate different tasks. State licensure, privacy, fairness, patient trust and evaluation of outcomes determine adoption, with some routine intake roles at risk.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn research methods and psychometric validity before using automated summaries.
- Practice supervised clinical interviewing and ethical decision-making appropriate to credential level.
- Compare tools on synthetic or public data, checking bias and quality..
