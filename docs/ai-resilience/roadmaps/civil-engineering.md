Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Civil engineers** — stable career ID `civil-engineers`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `17-2051`.

General civil-engineering task evidence; transportation and water/wastewater are named O*NET specialties without separate Matrix weights.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 17-2051.00 Civil Engineers; 17-2051.01 Transportation Engineers; 17-2051.02 Water/Wastewater Engineers.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 380,600 jobs in 2025; +6.4% projected change, 2025–35; 22,700 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 17-2051](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

Drafting, estimates and model interpretation can be assisted; field observations, structural judgment, constructability and public safety remain central. NCEES's responsible-charge policy keeps professional responsibility with licensees. Existing design software, sensors and construction automation are not synonymous with GenAI. ASCE's 2026 AI RACE guidance identifies code interpretation, professional judgment and verification as continuing human responsibilities; it is normative, not a prevalence statistic.

**Independent sources:** [Policy Manual, PS 6.10 Responsible Use of AI in Engineering and Surveying](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Policy-manual_August-2025_web.pdf), [Guidance on Improving Cybersecurity at Drinking Water and Wastewater Systems](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-08/epa-guidance-on-improving-cybersecurity-at-drinking-water-and-wastewater-systems-1.pdf), [The AI Jobs Transition Framework](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/the-ai-jobs-transition-framework_report.pdf), [AI Roadmap for Application in Civil Engineering (AI RACE)](https://www.asce.org/initiatives/ai-race).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

With authorized CAD/BIM and document access, agents may assemble routine alternatives, quantity documents and review checklists. The same engineer could handle more projects, but infrastructure needs, public procurement, fieldwork and review constraints limit direct substitution. Junior drafting must still teach engineering reasoning.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Build and independently check a design with code references.
- Seek field inspection or construction experience and keep an error log.
- Pursue FE/PE steps with supervision and document model assumptions..
