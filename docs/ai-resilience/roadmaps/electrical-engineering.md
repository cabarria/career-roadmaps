# Electrical Engineering: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Electrical engineers** — stable career ID `electrical-engineers`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `17-2071`.

Electrical engineers; electronics engineers (including many chip/RF roles) are a separate SOC.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 17-2071.00 Electrical Engineers.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 199,700 jobs in 2025; +9.9% projected change, 2025–35; 11,400 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 17-2071](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

Code and design assistance can help with documentation and simulation workflows. The software survey is indirect evidence only; established EDA, control algorithms and optimization are separate from GenAI. NCEES identifies continuing oversight and responsibility for licensed work; practical hardware tests are still needed. Yu et al.'s 2026 complex-chip benchmark reports a leading tested model completed about 31% of its design-generation problems; this is evidence of limitations on bounded tasks, not a worker productivity estimate.

**Independent sources:** [Policy Manual, PS 6.10 Responsible Use of AI in Engineering and Surveying](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Policy-manual_August-2025_web.pdf), [The AI Jobs Transition Framework](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/the-ai-jobs-transition-framework_report.pdf), [2026 Developer Survey: AI](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai), [ChipBench: A Next-Step Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Performance in AI-Aided Chip Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21448).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents may help with design-space search, firmware and verification documents; more complex chips, energy systems and products could raise demand. Strict requirements on data access, timing, reliability and physical validation limit delegation, particularly in power and regulated systems. Entry tasks may shift from drafting toward checking.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Design and test a small electrical system with measured-versus-predicted results.
- Learn simulation, instrumentation and verification fundamentals.
- Seek hardware or field placements and practice documented safety reviews..
