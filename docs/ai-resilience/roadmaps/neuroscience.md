# Neuroscience: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Medical scientists, except epidemiologists** — stable career ID `medical-scientists`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `19-1042`.

Medical scientists except epidemiologists include non-neuroscience work; exclude many clinicians, engineers and data roles.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 19-1042.00 Medical Scientists, Except Epidemiologists.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 181,000 jobs in 2025; +12.6% projected change, 2025–35; 10,500 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 19-1042](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

Cross-disciplinary research survey supports use of AI in research workflows, not neuroscience prevalence. Data and signal analysis can leverage non-generative ML as well as code assistance; NIH governance stresses disclosure and confidentiality. Human-subject protocols, measurement quality, independent interpretation and clinical validation remain important.

**Independent sources:** [ExplanAItions 2025–2026: Evolution of AI in Research](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/about-us/ai/study), [Guidelines for the Conduct of Research, ninth edition](https://oir.nih.gov/system/files/media/file/2026-09/guidelines-conduct_research.pdf), [Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents may reduce routine data cleaning and manuscript drafts; improved access to large datasets may create further research questions. Funding, ethics approvals, device validation, quality of measurements and clinical adoption constrain results. Clinical neuropsychology follows a separate licensed pathway.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn statistics, measurement and reproducible analysis with public datasets.
- Seek supervised research and learn human-subject ethics and privacy.
- Practice defending a research finding with limitations and independent checks..
