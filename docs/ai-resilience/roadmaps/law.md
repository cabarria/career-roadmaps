Lawyers, not judges, paralegals, clerks or legal academics.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 23-1011.00 Lawyers.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 863,700 jobs in 2025; +4.7% projected change, 2025–35; 28,700 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 23-1011](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

A preregistered evaluation found >17% hallucination rates in two tested specialized legal-research providers, despite retrieval. ABA Opinion 512 emphasizes competence, confidentiality, verification and supervision. Discovery has long involved non-GenAI automation; drafts and case search are increasingly assisted, not authoritative.

**Independent sources:** [Hallucination-Free? ## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents may produce more contract and discovery workflows; billing and junior associate staffing may shift, but litigation strategy, negotiation, client counsel, evidence and court responsibility persist. Cheaper services could expand access; confidential data and state-specific professional duties constrain use.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn research and citation validation on primary legal materials.
- Practice fact development, negotiation and oral advocacy through clinics.
- Seek supervised drafting experience and learn ethical AI data policies..
