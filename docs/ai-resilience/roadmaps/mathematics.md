# Mathematics: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Mathematicians** — stable career ID `mathematicians`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `15-2021`.

Mathematicians by job title, not statisticians, actuaries, quants, or all math degree holders.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 15-2021.00 Mathematicians.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 2,200 jobs in 2025; +0.6% projected change, 2025–35; 100 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 15-2021](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

A vendor-reported, externally graded model scored 35/42 at IMO 2025; a 2026 vendor report describes selected expert-directed research problems. Both demonstrate capability, not general autonomous mathematical research. Routine calculation, symbolic systems and formal proof checking predate GenAI; problem definitions, rigorous verification and significance still matter.

**Independent sources:** [Gemini Deep Think gold-medal standard at IMO](https://deepmind.google/blog/advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-officially-achieves-gold-medal-standard-at-the-international-mathematical-olympiad/), [Accelerating Mathematical and Scientific Discovery with Gemini Deep Think](https://deepmind.google/blog/accelerating-mathematical-and-scientific-discovery-with-gemini-deep-think/), [Generative AI for Economic Research: LLMs Learn to Collaborate and Reason](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33198).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Reasoning agents could explore more conjectures, draft proofs and implement calculations. Verification costs and false arguments may rise; more math applications could expand demand, but pure-math PhD positions depend on resources and faculty structure. Quant and actuarial work have separate responsibility.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn rigorous proof writing and counterexample construction unaided.
- Practice independent checking or formal proof tools and cite verified results.
- Build applied statistics/modeling projects with assumptions and validation..
