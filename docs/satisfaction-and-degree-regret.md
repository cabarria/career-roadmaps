# Career happiness, job satisfaction, and degree regret

The Career Metrics page (`/careers?metric=satisfaction`) opens Job satisfaction with CareerExplorer selected. The dataset picker lists CareerExplorer, NORC’s General Social Survey, and PayScale in that order. Degree regret is a separate comparison by surveyed major field. Each survey keeps its original scale and category labels where they differ from the roadmap occupations.

| Dataset | Coverage in the comparison | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CareerExplorer ongoing survey, checked November 24, 2026 | 28 survey categories linked to the 28 roadmaps | Average career happiness on the original 1–5 scale. The survey pages describe an ongoing participant survey, with geography and the overall respondent count for each career unspecified. Each comparison row links to its source page. [CareerExplorer directory](https://www.careerexplorer.com/careers/) |
| NORC General Social Survey, 2004–2024 | 6 featured occupations | Weighted percentage of employed U.S. respondents who were very or moderately satisfied, with 95% confidence intervals and respondent counts. [Coverage and methods](job-satisfaction.md) |
| PayScale, June 2013–June 2015 | 22 survey categories linked to the roadmaps | Percentage of respondents who were extremely or fairly satisfied with their jobs. Occupation definitions are from the survey period. [Full table](https://www.payscale.com/data-packages/most-and-least-meaningful-jobs/full-list) · [Methodology](https://www.payscale.com/data-packages/most-and-least-meaningful-jobs/methodology) |
| ZipRecruiter, January–March 2026 | 19 major fields linked to the roadmaps | Percentage of surveyed recent graduates who regretted their major. The figures refer to the class of 2025 cohort, with 1,500 respondents across all majors. The report also surveyed a separate cohort of rising graduates. [Report](https://www.ziprecruiter-research.org/economic-insights-research/grad-survey-2026) · [Chart data](https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/2GSs3/1/dataset.csv) |

## How the source checks changed the supplied figures

- **CareerExplorer.** Family practitioners have a direct rating of **3.1/5**. The supplied **3.4/5** described doctors overall. Where a supplied rating described a different category, the comparison uses the source label. Examples include biochemists for Molecular Biology, scientists for Neuroscience, and professors for Philosophy. The Career.io **4.07/5** data scientist figure is a separate analysis of employer reviews on Glassdoor. [Family practitioners](https://www.careerexplorer.com/careers/family-practitioner/satisfaction/) · [Career.io methodology](https://career.io/career-advice/careers-that-make-americans-happiest)
- **PayScale.** Civil engineers reported **71% high satisfaction** and **68% high meaning**. The full table also supplies direct historical satisfaction figures for architects (**70%**), graphic designers (**64%**), lawyers (**68%**), and registered nurses (**71%**). Social worker specialties range from **59% to 83%** across four categories. The comparison uses the GSS dataset for a single estimate covering social workers as a group. The **83%** for PayScale’s residual postsecondary teaching category has a different scope from philosophy and religion teaching. [PayScale full table](https://www.payscale.com/data-packages/most-and-least-meaningful-jobs/full-list)
- **ZipRecruiter.** The 2026 values for engineering (**15.9%**), computer science, information technology, or data science (**19.8%**), education or teaching (**16.4%**), and nursing or allied health (**14.6%**) are point estimates for the named categories. The 2022 job-seeker results cited for context were **29%** for engineering, **28%** for computer and information sciences, **61%** for education, and **31%** for nursing. The cohorts and some category definitions differ. [2026 chart data](https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/2GSs3/1/dataset.csv) · [2022 ZipRecruiter report](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/blog/regret-free-college-majors/) · [2022 figures](https://www.goacta.org/2024/08/oh-the-humanities-can-you-guess-the-most-regretted-college-majors/)

Degree regret is stored once per survey major field. Roadmap links identify related study or career paths. Engineering’s **15.9%** covers the surveyed engineering disciplines together, and **46.3%** covers political science, international relations, and public policy together. The Law roadmap is linked because those majors are possible undergraduate paths. Medicine and Pharmacy link to the survey’s combined medicine, pharmacy, or dentistry category (**11.8%**). The survey’s **29.8%** category is public health or health administration. [ZipRecruiter survey](https://www.ziprecruiter-research.org/economic-insights-research/grad-survey-2026) · [Chart data](https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/2GSs3/1/dataset.csv)

## Reading the figures together

- Registered nurses reported **80% high meaning** in PayScale’s survey and **2.7/5 career happiness** in CareerExplorer’s survey. The figures come from different respondents and measure different aspects of work. [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/data-packages/most-and-least-meaningful-jobs/full-list) · [CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/careers/registered-nurse/satisfaction/)
- CareerExplorer reports **4.1/5 career happiness** and **2.5/5 pay satisfaction** for writers. The survey asks about these separately. [CareerExplorer](https://www.careerexplorer.com/careers/writer/satisfaction/)
