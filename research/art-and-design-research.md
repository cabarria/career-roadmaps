# Art and Design: Research Notes for the Field Roadmap

**Field:** Art and Design, the US BFA/BA/BS in studio art and design (graphic, UX and product, illustration, animation, industrial design, fine art) and the careers it opens (US-centered)
**Compiled:** 2026-11-23

## TL;DR

- The BFA is the professional undergraduate degree. The College Art Association (CAA) guideline says at least 70 credits (or 60 percent) in the specialization, including 12 to 18 credit hours of art history, with professional studies no more than 75 percent of the total (S20). A hiring decision rests on the portfolio. BLS says portfolios are essential for artists and expects industrial designers to show an electronic portfolio (S2, S5).
- The MFA is the terminal degree for studio artists and designers (S19, S21). Most design jobs need only a bachelor's. BLS lists a bachelor's as the typical entry education for graphic designers, art directors, special effects artists and animators, and industrial designers (S1, S3, S4, S5).
- BLS May 2025 medians: graphic designers $62,960; art directors $114,850; special effects artists and animators $102,030; industrial designers $83,910; craft and fine artists $55,290; curators $63,420 (S1 to S6).
- Projected 2025-35 growth is negative or flat for most of the field: graphic designers -2%, craft and fine artists -1%, animators 0%, industrial designers 2%, art directors 4% (S1 to S5). BLS now says AI-based design tools are projected to reduce the need for graphic designers (S1).
- Screen-based product design is the best-paid mainstream route found. A Fast Company analysis of job listings found product designers offered about $70,000 at entry, $149,850 at four to five years, and $197,579 at eight or more years (S32). Salary.com's product designer average is $112,352 (S33).
- Fine art is mostly self-employed work: 58% of craft and fine artists are self-employed, and 63% of animators and effects artists are (S2, S4).
- Graduate funding varies widely. Notre Dame's MFA pays full tuition plus a $28,500 stipend (S15). A new federal earnings test can cut Direct Loan eligibility for master's programs whose graduates do not out-earn bachelor's holders, with the first possible designations in the 2028-29 award year (S9). The only field-level figure found (44% of arts master's programs failing) is unconfirmed and is not used (S14).
- Studio-art PhDs are rare in the US. Interdisciplinary design PhDs (Northeastern) and a praxis-based PhD (Texas Tech) exist (S27, S28).

- **The BLS pages changed between data years.** The first JSX used May 2024 figures: graphic designers $61,300, art directors $111,040, special effects artists and animators $99,800, industrial designers $79,450, and craft and fine artists $56,260. The May 2025 pages give different values. Graphic designer projected growth moved from +2% to -2%, and craft and fine artists from "flat" to -1%. The pages read do not explain the shifts, so no cause is claimed.
- **BLS on AI.** The graphic designer page says automated design tools such as AI are projected to make designers more productive and reduce the need for them, while a growing digital presence still requires designers for layouts and branding (S1). The craft and fine artists page says AI-generated illustrations "may compete with illustrators" (S2). The animator page says AI may dampen demand (S4, earlier read).
- **Self-employment.** Graphic designers 20% (S1), craft and fine artists 58% (S2), special effects artists and animators 63% (S4, earlier read).
- **Product design pay.** Fast Company's listing analysis (published 2025-06-23): UX and product designers both start near $70,000. At four to five years of required experience, UX designers were offered about $123,720 and product designers $149,850. At eight or more years, UX designers about $153,920 and product designers $197,579, which is about 28% more for product designers (S32). Salary.com (data as of 2026-11-01): average and median $112,352, 25th to 75th percentile $103,541 to $124,989, 90th percentile $136,494. By experience: under 1 year $78,577, 1 to 2 years $89,121, 2 to 4 years $111,422, 5 to 8 years $136,046, over 8 years $159,358 (S33). The two sources differ because one measures advertised salaries for specific experience bands and the other averages reported pay.
- **Creative director pay.** Indeed's average for creative directors is $145,897 (updated 2026-10-29), senior creative director $136,694, associate creative director $120,829, and the highest-paying companies $312,750 (S35). These are aggregator averages from job postings. No BLS figure for creative directors was found.
- **Industrial designer pay against inflation.** The Adobe "Economic State of Creative Professions" report shows nominal pay for commercial and industrial designers up 25.0% from 2015 to 2025 and real pay down 8.0% (S34).
- **BFA structure.** CAA: a minimum of 70 credits (or 60 percent) in the area of specialization, including 12 to 18 credit hours of art history; professional studies no more than 75 percent of the total; programmatic evaluation usually uses portfolio review (S20). Institutional curricula examined: SVA BFA Design, USC Roski, WashU Sam Fox, Northeastern Design BFA, George Mason BFA (S22 to S26).
- **MFA as terminal degree.** The MFA became the "terminal degree" for artists in the mid-20th century, with Yale, Iowa, and CalArts positioning it as credential and prestige mark (S19). A program-marketing page describes the MFA as a terminal degree for professional artists, studio-focused, against an MA that is writing-focused (S36, low).
- **MFA funding.** Notre Dame's MFA in Studio Art and Design: three years (60 credits), full tuition waivers for all admitted students, a $28,500 annual stipend, teaching opportunities in years two and three; article dated 2026-01-08 (S15). UConn's funded MFA offers tuition remission and a health-coverage subsidy, no stipend figure on the page (S16). Funding lists exist at EDsmart and Best Value Schools (S17, S18). Search excerpts also showed a $30,000 fellowship plus a $4,000 summer stipend at one program and a teaching assistant salary of $29,125 at another, but the schools were not identified in my notes, so those figures are not used. No unfunded-tuition total was rechecked, so the first JSX's "$100,000+" claim was removed.
- **Debt and program supply.** Hyperallergic's essay "The Art School Debt Trap" argues that MFA programs are marketed as gateways to success and that the fine print differs (S19, opinion).
- **Earnings test ("Do No Harm").** The Education Department's fact sheet says the rule is effective July 1, 2026, with the first earnings-test calculation in early 2027 for the 2027-28 award year. Programs failing in both 2027 and 2028 could be designated low-earning from the 2028-29 award year, and programs failing two out of three consecutive years lose Direct Loan eligibility (S9). Graduate programs must show graduates earn more than bachelor's holders (S9, S13). The fact sheet gives no share of failing master's programs and does not mention arts programs. IBTimes reports that 4% of master's programs were projected to fail overall, attributes that to the Department's own January analysis (which predates the final rule), lists fine arts and music among the most exposed fields without a percentage, and gives 17.7% for bachelor's graphic communications and 12% for film, video, and photographic arts (S12). The rule was published in the Federal Register on 2026-07-01 (S11).
- **Studio doctorates.** Northeastern offers a PhD in Interdisciplinary Design and Media (S27). Texas Tech offers a praxis-based doctoral program in fine arts (S28). UIUC offers a PhD in art education (S29). The Chronicle reports an ongoing debate over whether studio artists need a doctorate (S30).

## Undergraduate

### Curriculum

- **Structure.** The BFA is the professional degree: CAA's guideline sets at least 70 credits (about 60 percent) in the specialization, including 12 to 18 hours of art history, with professional studies capped at 75 percent so that at least a quarter of credits sit outside it (S20). A BA pairs art with broader study, and a BS adds technical content (S20 to S26, program pages vary).
- **Foundation year.** SVA, Roski, WashU and others open with shared first-year studios in drawing, 2D and 3D design, color, and time-based media (S22 to S24). Details differ by school.
- **Concentrations.** Graphic or communication design, illustration, industrial design, media and animation, and fine art are typical declarations (S22 to S26).
- **Art history.** 12 to 18 credit hours under CAA's BFA guideline (S20).

### Skills

- Portfolio review is the usual evaluation mechanism for BFAs (S20). BLS says portfolios are essential for artists (S2) and that industrial designers are expected to show an electronic portfolio, with CAD skills (2D CADD for sketching, 3D CAD for models) used in the work (S5).

### Tools and extracurriculars

- BLS names CAD software for industrial designers (S5). No source retained in these notes gave a ranked tool list for graphic or UX work. Fast Company notes that UX and product designers use Figma more than other designers do (S32).
- BLS lists a bachelor's as typical for art directors with five or more years of related work experience (S3).

### Not found

- Which studio courses are hardest. The critique ("crit") is described in program materials as central, but no source ranks it as a weeder, so the JSX frames it as a defining experience rather than a measured one.
- Evidence that employers "consistently rank" portfolios above GPA or school name. BLS says portfolios are essential, which supports the portfolio claim but not the ranking.

## Master's

- **MFA.** Studio-based, usually two to three years, ending in a thesis body of work and exhibition (S19, S36). Treated as the terminal degree (S19, S21).
- **MA versus MFA.** The MA is writing-focused and the MFA studio-focused (S36, low).
- **Design master's.** MDes and MA programs exist in design and design research. No outcome data was found.
- **Funding.** See Key Findings (S15 to S18). Notre Dame pays full tuition and $28,500 (S15).
- **Earnings test.** See Key Findings (S9, S12, S13).
- **Not found.** Placement or earnings data for studio MFAs, acceptance rates, and unfunded tuition totals.

## PhD / Research

- **Studio PhDs are rare.** Northeastern's Interdisciplinary Design and Media PhD (S27) and Texas Tech's praxis-based doctoral program (S28) are examples; a Sculpture magazine listing covers others (S31). UIUC offers an art education PhD (S29).
- **Debate.** The Chronicle reports the discipline is divided over whether artists need a doctorate (S30).
- **Not found.** Placement outcomes, funding norms, and duration for studio doctorates.

## Tools and AI

- **Standard tools.** CAD for industrial design (S5). Figma for UX and product design (S32). The first JSX also named Adobe Creative Cloud, After Effects, Blender, Procreate and similar. These are common knowledge but no retained source ranked them, so the roadmap describes them without ranking.
- **AI and employment.** BLS: AI tools projected to reduce the need for graphic designers (S1); AI-generated illustration may compete with illustrators (S2); AI may dampen demand for animators (S4, earlier read). Fast Company says UX design is more vulnerable to AI than product design (S32, subscriber excerpt, the reasoning was not read in full).
- **Not used.** An AI-risk calculator's "88% displacement risk" for graphic designers (S37, low), a claim that about 21% of film, television, and animation jobs would face generative-AI pressure by 2026 (S38, low, original source not found), a "60% of design programs embed AI" claim (S39, low), and a "33% fall in graphic design postings in 2025" figure whose primary source was not retained.

## Career Paths

### 1. UX and product design

- **Titles:** UX designer, product designer, senior product designer, design lead, director of design.
- **Reality:** The best-paid mainstream route found. Fast Company's listing analysis shows advertised pay rising from about $70,000 at entry to $149,850 (product) or $123,720 (UX) at four to five years, and to $197,579 or $153,920 at eight or more years. UX roles are more exposed to AI than product roles in that analysis. A bachelor's and a portfolio are the usual entry; no source retained here measured how crowded entry-level hiring is.
- **Salary:** About $70K entry, $124K to $150K at four to five years, $154K to $198K at eight or more years (Fast Company, 2025). Salary.com average $112,352 (2026).
- **Sources:** S32, S33.

### 2. Graphic and visual design

- **Titles:** Junior designer, graphic designer, senior designer, art director.
- **Reality:** The largest design occupation (253,100 jobs) and projected to shrink 2% from 2025 to 2035, with about 16,000 annual openings, all from replacement. BLS expects AI tools to reduce the need for designers while branding and layout work continue. Twenty percent are self-employed.
- **Salary:** Median $62,960; bottom 10% under $39,520; top 10% over $104,910 (BLS, May 2025).
- **Sources:** S1.

### 3. Illustration and concept art

- **Titles:** Illustrator, concept artist, senior illustrator, art lead.
- **Reality:** BLS folds illustrators into craft and fine artists and says AI-generated illustrations may compete with them. No illustrator-specific pay or freelance rate was found. Self-employment in the group is 58%.
- **Salary:** Craft and fine artists median $55,290 (BLS, May 2025). No illustrator-specific range found.
- **Sources:** S2.

### 4. Animation, motion, and VFX

- **Titles:** Motion designer, animator, VFX artist, animation director.
- **Reality:** Median pay is $102,030 but the range runs from under $58,600 to over $177,110, projected growth is 0%, and 63% of workers are self-employed. BLS says AI may dampen demand. Industry geography and cyclicality claims in the first JSX were not supported and were removed.
- **Salary:** Median $102,030; bottom 10% under $58,600; top 10% over $177,110 (BLS, May 2025).
- **Sources:** S4 (earlier read).

### 5. Industrial and product design

- **Titles:** Junior industrial designer, industrial designer, senior designer, design manager.
- **Reality:** A small field (35,400 jobs, about 2,800 openings a year, +2% growth) that expects a bachelor's in industrial design, architecture, engineering, or fine arts, an electronic portfolio, and CAD skills. Adobe's analysis shows real pay 8.0% below its 2015 level in 2025. BLS names no AI effect on this occupation.
- **Salary:** Median $83,910; bottom 10% under $53,460; top 10% over $139,770 (BLS, May 2025).
- **Sources:** S5, S34.

### 6. Art direction and creative leadership

- **Titles:** Senior designer, art director, creative director, chief creative officer.
- **Reality:** Art directors need a bachelor's and five or more years of related experience. There are 140,200 jobs, growth is 4%, and about 12,200 openings a year. Creative director pay is only available from aggregators.
- **Salary:** Art directors median $114,850; bottom 10% under $64,900; top 10% over $213,050 (BLS). Creative directors average about $145,897 (Indeed, 2026, aggregator).
- **Sources:** S3, S35.

### 7. Fine art and studio practice

- **Titles:** Working artist, exhibiting artist, represented artist, teaching artist.
- **Reality:** Median $55,290, growth -1%, 53,400 jobs, and 58% self-employed. BLS says portfolios are essential and that most fine artists pursue postsecondary education. Gallery and grant claims in the JSX describe common practice and were not tied to a source here.
- **Salary:** Median $55,290; bottom 10% under $31,490; top 10% over $131,810 (BLS, May 2025).
- **Sources:** S2.

### 8. Art education and museums

- **Titles:** Teaching artist, K-12 art teacher, professor, curator, museum director.
- **Reality:** K-12 teaching needs a bachelor's and a state license (S8). College teaching with an MFA is the common route (S21). BLS curator growth is 5% (the archivist, curator, and museum worker group is 4%), and curators typically need a master's. No art-specific K-12 teacher pay was found.
- **Salary:** Curators median $63,420 (BLS, May 2025). High school teachers, all subjects, median $72,040 (BLS, May 2025).
- **Sources:** S6, S8, S21.

## Uncertainties and Regional Variation

- **Aggregator versus BLS pay.** Creative director and product designer pay come from job-posting aggregators (Indeed, Salary.com) or one analysis of listings (Fast Company). BLS has no product designer or creative director line, so no official figure exists to check against.
- **Geography.** Pay for design roles varies by metro area and by whether the employer is a technology company. No regional breakdown was gathered. Outside the US, degree structures and funding differ.
- **Earnings-test impact on arts programs.** The only field-level figure found (44% of arts master's programs at risk) came from an article about the earlier proposal (S14, low) and is unconfirmed against the final rule. The Department's fact sheet gives no field breakdown (S9). Actual effects depend on 2027 calculations.
- **Two BLS pages not reopened.** Animator and curator figures rest on an earlier read of the pages (S4, S6).
- **Fetch summaries.** Figures from fetches are model-extracted and should be rechecked on the page.
- **MFA funding.** Only a handful of programs were checked. Funding at other programs, and unfunded tuition totals, were not.
- **Creative-sector layoffs and hiring.** No primary data on posting trends was retained.
