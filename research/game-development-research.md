# The Game Development and Design Career Roadmap (USA): Undergraduate to PhD, Stage 1 Research Synthesis

Prepared 2026-11-23. Provenance note: this file was written after the first version of the roadmap was built. It combines the searches run for that build with follow-up searches and page fetches run on 2026-11-23 to close gaps. The roadmap was then revised so that its claims trace to this file. Claims that could not be sourced were removed or softened. Where a figure comes only from a search-result excerpt and not from a full page, the excerpt is the basis.

## TL;DR

- A game development degree works as a portfolio-building vehicle more than a credential gate. Program descriptions center on team capstones and finished games, and none of the career sources reviewed lists a studio role that requires a master's degree.
- The entry market is hard. Estimates put roughly 40,000 to 45,000 game jobs cut from 2022 to mid-2025. In GDC's 2026 survey, 28% of respondents (33% in the US) were laid off in the past two years, and 74% of surveyed students are concerned about their job prospects.
- Pay varies more by discipline than by degree. Programming is the best-paid track in the sources (Levels.fyi median total compensation of about $190,776 for video game software engineers at tracked companies; Glassdoor game developer average of $96,695). Game designer averages sit near $98,000 (Glassdoor), while QA and art roles start around $40,000 to $60,000.
- Generative AI is contested in the field. GDC 2026: 36% of professionals use it, and 52% say it is having a negative impact on the industry, up from 30% a year earlier and 18% the year before.
- Indie income is a power law. One analysis puts the median 2025 Steam release at about $249 gross, while the best-selling indie titles earned hundreds of millions.
- Serious games and simulation is a distinct employer set (defense, healthcare, training) with its own requirements, including U.S. citizenship or clearance eligibility for some roles. No broad salary survey for it was found.

## Key Findings

1. **Degrees come in three flavors.** Examples: BS programs (Iowa State's multidisciplinary BS in Game Design; RIT's BS), BFA programs with a studio model (UConn, NYU), and BA programs (Quinnipiac, UTSA). Harrisburg University's BS says it does not require extensive coding. [S1, S3, S5, S6, S7, S8, S11]
2. **The capstone is the portfolio centerpiece.** Iowa State's capstone is a 6-credit, team-based project from concept to polished prototype. UConn's BFA ends in a year-long senior project. [S1, S6]
3. **The master's is professional and optional.** CMU's Master of Entertainment Technology (MET) is a two-year terminal degree. USC splits design (MFA in Interactive Media) from technical work (MS in Computer Science, Games). Costs run from tens of thousands to well over $100,000 by one low-reliability ranking site. Georgia Tech's Digital Media MS offers part-time assistantships, so some funding exists. [S9, S10, S11, S16]
4. **Game-focused PhDs are few and small.** Georgia Tech's Digital Media PhD admits 3 to 5 candidates a year and guarantees funding. UC Santa Cruz's Computational Media PhD is recruiting for 2026-27. [S14, S15, S18]
5. **The job market contracted sharply.** About 40,000 to 45,000 jobs were lost from 2022 to mid-2025, with a peak in early 2024. Layoffs slowed in 2025 and hiring began to match them, but entry-level competition remains severe. [S22, S28, S29, S30]
6. **AI sentiment is moving negative, and usage is mixed.** Primary GDC data and a separate Game Developer panel point in somewhat different directions on adoption (see Tools and AI). [S22, S24]
7. **Unreal now leads engine use.** 42% of developers name Unreal as their primary engine, versus 30% for Unity. [S22]
8. **Indie outcomes are extremely skewed.** Roughly 20,000 games launched on Steam in 2025; about 300 passed $1 million in revenue. [S61, S62]
9. **Salary sources disagree widely.** Averages for game designers range from about $72,000 to about $110,000 across aggregators, and one source (ZipRecruiter) was excluded as implausible. See Uncertainties. [S33 to S40]

## Undergraduate (Years 1 to 4)

**Program types.**

- Iowa State's BS in Game Design draws on four colleges (Design, Engineering, Human Sciences, Liberal Arts and Sciences). [S1]
- Harrisburg University's BS in Game Design emphasizes teamwork and conflict resolution and says students can succeed "without requiring extensive coding." Its core sequence runs from analog prototyping and ideation to principles and practice to designing for a target demographic with playtesting. [S3]
- UConn's BFA in Game Design uses a shared Digital Media and Design core (plus Drawing 1), a junior core of Interactive Storytelling and 3D Virtual Worlds, specializations (3D modeling for games, game system design, multiplayer development, advanced game scripting), and a year-long BFA senior project. [S6]
- Quinnipiac's BA recommends a minor or double major in a complementary field such as computer science or interactive design, and requires experiential learning. [S5]
- UTSA's multidisciplinary BA lists C#, C++, or Python for gameplay scripting. [S7]
- RIT's BS requires two co-op blocks and offers a combined BS/MS in as little as five years. [S8]
- Full Sail runs an accelerated program. [S2]

**Core curriculum themes.** Game design fundamentals (formal and dramatic elements, mechanics, narrative); systems design using spreadsheets to record and manipulate game data; game mechanics analysis; game balancing; UX design; digital storytelling; 2D and 3D modeling and animation; programming (Java, C++, C# named by US News). [S1, S2, S3, S4]

**Weeder courses.** No source reviewed identifies notoriously difficult courses for game programs. This was not found. The roadmap therefore does not name any.

**Hands-on skills.** Analog prototyping, playtesting with player data, team roles and conflict resolution, iterative design on a team capstone. [S1, S3]

**Foundational tools.** Entry-level game engines (Harrisburg), Unity, Unreal Engine, and Autodesk software (Research.com), and C#, C++, or Python for scripting (UTSA). [S3, S7, S66] Primary engine shares from GDC 2026: Unreal 42%, Unity 30%, Godot 11% among newer indie developers; Unreal is more common at AA (59%) and AAA (47%) studios; 54% of developers at older indie studios still use Unity. [S22]

**Extracurriculars.**

- RIT's co-ops, and its Creative Industry Day connecting students with employers. [S8]
- Student clubs: UConn lists a Game Development Club and an Escape Room Club, and Big East gaming competition. [S6]
- A student studio at RIT released The Salesman, which sold 8,000 copies at $5. [S8]
- Game jams are an active research subject: one PhD project studies how jam formats shape the way people develop games. [S21]
- USC students describe a bind: employers want experienced candidates, and few entry-level or intern positions exist to gain that experience. [S30]
- Practitioners in Q&A threads call a bachelor's degree "just a checkmark" and say some game design degrees are overpriced. This is opinion, not survey data. [S65]

## Master's (Years ~5 to 7)

**Program types.**

- CMU Entertainment Technology Center: Master of Entertainment Technology (MET), a terminal two-year degree run jointly by the College of Fine Arts and the School of Computer Science. Courses include Building Virtual Worlds, Improvisational Acting, Fundamentals of Entertainment Technology, and Visual Story. Electives span Game Design, Interactive Storytelling, Themed Entertainment, and Leadership and Innovation. CMU also offers an undergraduate Game Design Minor through IDeATe. [S11] CMU graduates overall are routinely hired by companies including Amazon, Epic Systems, Apple, Google, Microsoft, ESPN, IBM, Meta, and 2K Games; the source does not break out MET graduates, and Epic Systems is a healthcare software company, not Epic Games. [S12]
- USC: MFA in Interactive Media (School of Cinematic Arts), MS in Computer Science (Game Development, Viterbi School of Engineering), and an MA in Cinematic Arts with an emphasis in Media Arts, Games and Health. USC's Los Angeles location is cited for access to internships, events, and companies including Sony, Blizzard, and Riot Games. [S10, S13]
- NYU Game Center offers a BFA and MFA in game design. [S11]
- Other programs named in the sources: SMU Guildhall, DigiPen (a for-profit school offering graduate and undergraduate degrees, founded with support from Nintendo), University of Utah EAE (Princeton Review ranked it #1 for undergraduate and #3 for graduate game design, per one source; Hakia calls Utah the top public program), UCF's Interactive Entertainment Academy, and SNHU. [S9, S13]
- RIT's combined BS/MS. [S8]
- Georgia Tech's Digital Media MS (see funding below). [S16]

**Funding and cost.** Hakia estimates two-year totals of about $23,668 (public in-state), $60,000 (public out-of-state), $90,000 (private), and $36,000 (online), and says totals range from $47,336 at public universities to over $180,000 at elite private institutions. These are internally inconsistent and Hakia is a low-reliability source (its game development ranking page includes unrelated cybersecurity text), so treat them as rough. [S9] Georgia Tech's Digital Media MS students working as graduate assistants work about 13 hours a week for a stipend of $1,321 a month with tuition waived (mandatory fees still apply). [S16]

**Terminal or bridge.** CMU's MET is explicitly terminal. Whether most game master's graduates go on to PhDs was not researched. [S11]

**Advanced skills and tools.** Beyond program descriptions (team production, specialization tracks), no source gave advanced method or tool details specific to game master's programs.

## PhD / Research (Years ~8+)

**Georgia Tech Digital Media PhD.** Admits 3 to 5 new candidates a year. All PhD students are guaranteed a GTA or GRA: about 20 hours a week, a stipend of about $2,500 a month, tuition waived (students pay mandatory fees plus $25). GRE scores are not required. Applicants submit one to three computational artifacts showing creative and technical ability in expressive computing. Requirements include: core courses (LMC 6310 The Computer as an Expressive Medium, 6313 Principles of Interactive Design, 6316 Historical Approaches to New Media, 6399 Discovery and Invention); a project studio taken twice; a master's project or thesis (6 credits); pro-seminars; a minor concentration of three courses outside the school; a portfolio review; a three-part qualifying exam (two written parts covering two of four fields, HCI, STS, Design, and Media Studies, then a two-hour oral exam); a thesis proposal with prospectus; a public oral defense; and a 6-credit dissertation. The program page gives no typical time to degree, only a minimum of two semesters of full-time residency, and links to a 2021 handbook that may be out of date. [S14, S15, S17]

**UC Santa Cruz Computational Media.** Offers an MS and PhD. A 2016 call listed faculty research in generative game design, software and platform studies, interactive narrative and characters, assistive technology, games and emotion, social and embodied interaction, and personalized media. Faculty listed then included Katherine Isbister (HCI and games, emotion and social connection), Noah Wardrip-Fruin (digital fiction and games), Jim Whitehead (procedural generation of game levels), and Erin Robinson Swink (game designer; her educational game Fair Play was funded by an NIH Pathfinder Award). The department's current page says faculty are recruiting PhD students for 2026-27, describes Magy Seif El-Nasr's work on games and AI in education, and mentions a postdoctoral fellow, Linda Hirsch, who developed a flood-preparedness game. [S18]

**Research frontiers.** Frontiers' HCI research calls name: design and development of games and playful systems, tools for designing, developing, and studying games, player experience and games user research and game analytics, new interaction techniques and VR, social gaming, serious games and gamification, game-based learning and assessment, exergames in healthcare, VR and AR games, AI and deep learning in games, personalized and adaptive serious games, and ethics in serious game design. A CHI review of ten years of games research found games for a purpose to be the dominant form since 2010, and noted gaps in studies of developers, casual game design, and tools for game creation. [S19, S20] GDC's 2026 trends report names five trends: generative AI, co-development, dual monetization, funding and publishing pressure, and advocacy and accessibility concerns. [S23]

**AI in the research world.** A 2025 qualitative research synthesis (arXiv) reports that generative AI lowers entry barriers and widens participation for independent developers, and that industry ethnography records predominant skepticism in craft-intensive roles, with asset-class boundaries such as 3D models tempering early optimism. A PMC study of game design reports a democratizing effect for indie developers. [S25, S26]

**Where PhDs land.** Georgia Tech states graduates are prepared to work in industry, public service, and universities. [S14] No faculty hiring data or placement statistics were found. Wargaming has posted a UX Researcher role in its Center for Player Insights, which shows industry research roles exist. [S54]

## Tools and AI

**Standard tools.** Unity, Unreal Engine, Autodesk software, C#, C++, Python (see Undergraduate). [S7, S22, S66]

**GDC 2026 State of the Game Industry (primary source).** Survey of more than 2,300 game industry professionals, with a smaller survey of educators and students (sample size not given). 36% use generative AI as part of their job: 30% at game studios versus 58% at publishers, support teams, and marketing and PR firms. 52% say generative AI is having a negative impact on the industry, up from 30% last year and 18% the year before. 7% say positive impact, down from 13%. 28% were laid off in the past two years (33% in the US), and half said their current or most recent employer had layoffs in the past 12 months. [S22] The GDC trends report summary says developers want AI for planning and repetitive tasks, and that agentic AI could eventually take on bug triage, coding, and player support, but most developers want it to support rather than replace the development process. [S23]

**Game Developer Collective panel (separate source).** Reported use of generative AI rose from 24% in the second half of 2024, then declined about 7 percent after its 2025 peak. The share of panelists bullish on AI cutting costs fell from 27% (first half of 2025) to 21% (start of 2026), and the number expecting it to increase costs rose. In mid-2025, 47% worried AI would hurt game quality and 11% expected a positive impact. The article says "percent" without clarifying percentage points. [S24]

**Conflicting adoption figures.** A secondary blog cites a Google Cloud and Harris Poll survey of 615 developers in five countries finding 90% integrate AI into workflows. This is far higher than GDC's 36%, likely because of sample and definition differences, and it is a secondary source. It is not used in the roadmap. [S27]

## Career Paths

Salary figures are US annual figures. Each path lists the source behind the figure.

### 1. Game Design (Studio)

**Titles.** QA Tester, Associate Designer, Game or Level Designer, Senior Designer, Lead Designer, Creative Director. [S37]

**Reality.** Entry is tight (see layoffs). Common entry routes named in the sources: QA tester, level designer, freelance developer. [S37] The Salary.com title list includes Game Economy Designer ($92,338) and Technical Game Designer ($84,214). [S35] GDC survey respondents want AI to support planning and repetitive tasks. [S23] 33% of US respondents were laid off in two years; USC games students describe competing with experienced laid-off developers. [S22, S30]

**Salary.** Glassdoor average $97,981 (25th percentile $73,485, 75th $133,521, 90th $175,041; 1,735 salaries, June 2026); junior designer average $77,652 (25th $59,623, 75th $101,908; 47 salaries, May 2026); Salary.com junior average $76,713 (April 2026) and Lead Game Designer $138,223; Research.com junior $50,000 to $65,000, QA tester $40,000 to $55,000, mid-career $70,000 to $95,000, senior above $100,000; Payscale-based guide: entry about $46,000 to $62,000, lead roles often above $150,000. Roadmap range: $45k to $150k+ (QA and junior to lead). [S33, S34, S35, S36, S37]

### 2. Game Programming

**Titles.** Gameplay Programmer, Tools Programmer, Graphics or Engine Programmer, Senior Engineer, Technical Director. [S41, S45]

**Reality.** Levels.fyi lists a median total compensation of $190,776 for video game software engineers, but its sample skews toward large, high-paying companies. Glassdoor's game developer average is $96,695 (25th $72,587, 75th $129,815, 90th $168,258; 870 salaries, June 2026). Payscale's average for video game programmers is $71,132. Research.com: gameplay programmers $60,000 to $75,000 new graduates and $100,000 to $130,000 mid-career; game software engineers $65,000 to $80,000 rising to $110,000 to $140,000; graphics programmers $70,000 to $85,000 rising to $130,000 to $160,000; tools programmers $55,000 to $70,000 growing toward $120,000. A practitioner site puts entry-level around $44,000 and says programmers can move into web, app, and general software work. Research.com ties C++ and engine mastery to higher pay. [S37, S41, S42, S43, S44, S45] GDC trends coverage says agentic AI could eventually take on coding and bug triage. [S23]

**Salary.** Roadmap range: $55k to $190k+ (entry to the Levels.fyi median).

### 3. Game Art and Animation

**Titles.** Concept Artist, 3D or Character Artist, Environment Artist, Animator, Technical Artist, Art Director. [S37, S46]

**Reality.** Research.com entry pay: concept artist $45,000 to $60,000; technical artist $50,000 to $60,000. Payscale lists senior technical artists with games skills at an average of $122,000 (25th to 75th percentile $100,000 to $155,000), based on only 8 profiles last updated February 2024. A salary guide cites $80 to $150 an hour for specialized technical artist, tools programmer, and technical director contract work (January 2025, secondary). The qualitative synthesis finds skepticism toward generative AI strongest in craft-intensive roles, with 3D model generation tempering early optimism. [S25, S37, S48, S49, S50]

**Salary.** Roadmap range: $45k to $155k (senior technical art at the top).

### 4. Production and Project Management

**Titles.** Associate Producer, Producer, Senior Producer, Executive Producer, Studio Director. [S46]

**Reality.** Research.com says producers manage entire project lifecycles, budgets, and teams, with starting pay of $50,000 to $65,000 and mid-career pay of $95,000 to $125,000, and that producers and project managers usually have more consistent schedules than programmers and artists, though crunch can affect balance. Glassdoor: average $74,508 (25th $55,881, 75th $104,311, 90th $136,350; 139 salaries, June 2026); top-paying companies in Media and Communication include Riot Games and Blizzard Entertainment. [S46, S47]

**Salary.** Roadmap range: $50k to $135k+ (mid-career $95k to $125k).

### 5. Narrative Design and Writing

**Titles.** Game Writer, Narrative Designer, Senior Narrative Designer, Narrative Director. [S6, S31]

**Reality.** UConn teaches Interactive Storytelling as a junior core course. Research.com describes narrative design and writing as showing promising career development but relatively modest pay. ZipRecruiter's June 5, 2026 figure for narrative designers is an average of about $83,148, with most workers between $58,000 and $109,000, and a CD Projekt Red Boston posting at $118,000 to $196,000. A vendor guide (GrowthHQ) flags narrative and junior roles as the highest-risk roles in the AI era. [S6, S31, S46, S51]

**Salary.** Roadmap range: $58k to $109k (most workers).

### 6. Indie and Entrepreneurship

**Titles.** Solo Developer, Indie Studio Founder, Contract Developer, Live-Ops Operator.

**Reality.** About 20,000 games launched on Steam in 2025 and about 300 passed $1 million. One analysis (VG Insights and Alinea Analytics data, via Ziva) puts the median 2025 release at $249 gross ($174 after Valve's 30% cut), says 5,863 games earned over $100,000 per Valve at GDC 2026 but that analyst Simon Carless estimated only about 1,700 of those launched in 2025 (about 8.5% of new releases), puts a debut game's average at $120,000 and a third game's at $209,000, and says the top five indie releases earned over $500 million combined. A separate analysis (Steam Page Analyzer) estimates the median indie game's lifetime gross at $5,000 to $15,000. The Ziva analysis advises treating a first release as paid tuition, funding it from savings or a day job, and building an audience before a studio. Qualitative studies report AI lowers entry barriers for independent developers. [S25, S26, S61, S62, S63]

**Salary.** No typical salary. A GitNux page claims a median of $50,000 for full-time indies, but the page contains internally inconsistent statistics (for example "155% of indie devs are solo") and was excluded. [S64]

### 7. Serious Games and Simulation

**Titles.** Simulation Developer, Training Systems Designer, XR Developer, Serious Games Producer. [S55, S56, S58]

**Reality.** Serious games are games with a primary purpose other than entertainment, used in defense, education, healthcare, emergency management, and other sectors. HigherEchelon builds training products in Unity and Unreal for clients ranging from NFL teams to the U.S. Army and supports the Army Game Studio. Requirements can be restrictive: an Applied Intuition defense simulation role requires U.S. citizenship and eligibility for a Secret clearance, a master's in modeling and simulation, computer science, or software engineering (or equivalent experience), and 3+ years of C++; an Anduril training simulation role requires U.S. person status. Anduril's posted base range is $166,000 to $220,000 plus equity for 3+ years of experience, located in Santa Ana, CA. That is one defense-tech employer, not a market average. No broad salary survey was found. [S55, S56, S57, S58, S59, S60]

**Salary.** Roadmap range: one defense-tech posting at $166k to $220k base; otherwise no reliable range.

### 8. Games Research and Academia

**Titles.** Games User Researcher, Player Insights Analyst, Research Scientist, Professor of Game Design.

**Reality.** Doctoral programs are small (Georgia Tech: 3 to 5 admits a year). No faculty hiring data was found. Industry research roles exist (Wargaming's Center for Player Insights has posted a UX Researcher role). Pay data is thin: Comparably lists an average near $101,312 for games user researchers, last updated November 13, 2023. Levels.fyi's Wargaming UX Researcher figures (average total compensation of 88,800 to 103,000 euros) are for Cyprus and do not apply to US pay. [S14, S52, S53, S54]

**Salary.** Roadmap range: about $100k average for games user researchers (2023 data, thin).

### Editorial judgments (not measured figures)

The AI exposure badge on each career card is an editorial judgment informed by the sources, not a measured figure. Basis for each:

- Game Design, MODERATE: AI is used for planning, prototyping, and documentation; developers want it to support the process. [S23, S25]
- Game Programming, MODERATE: coding assistance appears in classroom and industry accounts, and trends coverage says agentic AI could eventually take on coding and bug triage. [S23, S25, S26]
- Game Art, HIGH: skepticism is strongest in craft-intensive roles, and concept generation is a common AI use, though 3D asset generation is less proven. [S25]
- Production, LOW: no source addresses it. Pure judgment.
- Narrative, HIGH: a vendor guide flags narrative and junior roles as highest risk. Weak source. [S31]
- Indie, AI as force multiplier: qualitative studies report lowered barriers for independent developers. [S25, S26]
- Serious Games, LOW: no source addresses it. Pure judgment.
- Research and Academia, LOW: no source addresses it. Pure judgment.

## Uncertainties and Regional Variation

**Salary conflicts.**

- Game designer averages differ by source: Glassdoor $97,981, Indeed $97,695 (308 postings, June 2026), Built In $110,667, Payscale about $71,708, Coursera citing a median total of $84,000. [S33, S36, S38, S39, S67]
- ZipRecruiter shows $149,118 for both "game designer" and "entry-level game designer" with identical 25th, 75th, and 90th percentiles of $155,000. That is implausible and was excluded. [S40]
- BLS data is available only for a proxy category (special effects artists and animators: median $99,800, 2% projected growth from 2024 to 2034, about 5,000 openings a year) and not for game designers specifically. Older BLS projections quoted by US News (2016 to 2026) are stale and were not used. [S4, S36]
- Payscale's senior technical artist figure rests on 8 profiles last updated February 2024. Comparably's user researcher figure was last updated November 2023. [S50, S52]

**Layoff counts.** Community trackers (via Game Developer, August 2025) say nearly 40,000; Wikipedia and GrowthHQ say about 45,000 from 2022 to July 2025. Annual tracker totals were 8,500 (2022), 10,500 (2023), 14,600 (2024), and about 4,000 for 2025 through July. [S28, S29, S31]

**Regional variation.**

- This research is US-focused.
- North America accounted for more than 70% of tracked 2025 layoffs, per The Game Business as cited by one source. [S32]
- Payscale game designer pay varies by metro: San Francisco about $88,490, San Jose about $83,524, New York City about $70,498. [S36]
- Levels.fyi Wargaming figures are Cyprus and in euros. [S53]
- Serious games roles may require U.S. citizenship or clearance. [S55, S56]

**Source reliability.**

- Hakia (master's costs) and GitNux (indie stats) are low-reliability aggregators. [S9, S64]
- aibuzz.blog and Jenova are secondary vendor blogs. [S27]
- GrowthHQ is a vendor blog. [S31]
- Quora answers are individual opinion. [S65]

**Not researched.**

- Which game courses are weeders.
- Typical PhD time to degree.
- Faculty hiring requirements and placement.
- Master's outcome and placement rates.

**General knowledge retained in the roadmap without a source.** Git and Perforce as common version control tools, and Blueprints as Unreal's visual scripting system. These are widely known but unverified here.
