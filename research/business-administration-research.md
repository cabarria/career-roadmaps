## TL;DR

- Business Administration is usually a BBA or BSBA (about 120 credits): a common business core (accounting, economics, statistics, finance, marketing, management, operations, law, information systems) plus a concentration. A bachelor's is fully employable, so the graduate stages are optional accelerants rather than gatekeepers.
- The MBA is the signature graduate degree, but most MBA students are working professionals. It is mostly self-funded (average about $60K, about $253K at top-ranked programs). GMAC data reported by Forbes puts the projected 2025 median MBA salary at $125K.
- Two different doctorates exist. The PhD runs about 4 to 7 years, is full-time, and is usually funded. The DBA runs about 3 years, is part-time, is self-funded, and leads to practice rather than tenure-track research.
- Faculty pay is high (AACSB 2025-26: accounting assistant professors about $170K, finance about $180K). The "chronic shortage" story is dated. AACSB's 2026 report calls the new-doctorate market "competitive" and notes hiring freezes at many institutions.
- AI is hitting the entry-level tier hardest. Stanford researchers report employment of 22 to 25 year olds fell 11% in high-AI-exposure occupations and rose 10% in low-exposure ones, mostly through reduced hiring (they do not claim proof of cause).
- Eight career destinations were researched. BLS base wages understate front-office finance, consulting and executive pay, so those lanes use practitioner and survey sources.

## Key Findings

- MBB consulting is about a 1% acceptance funnel. McKinsey's own figures (as relayed by its global managing partner on an HBR podcast, January 2026): more than 1,000,000 applications and 8,000 to 10,000 hires a year. BCG and Bain figures are estimates from a coaching firm.
- Two consulting pay numbers get confused. Bloomberg's $205,310 is the median starting salary plus bonus for Class of 2025 MBAs who took any consulting job. The MBB-specific figure is a $200,000 base for MBA hires starting in 2027 (Management Consulted, 120+ offer letters), with bonuses separate.
- "90% of startups fail" is not supported for businesses in general. BLS tracked private-sector establishments opened in March 2013: 79.6% still open after 1 year, 50.6% after 5 years, 34.7% after 10 years. BLS counts establishments (locations), not firms.
- BLS growth projections have moved to a 2025 to 2035 window. Financial managers are now projected at 10% (earlier 2024 to 2034 page: 15%). Logisticians are now 18%. Business teachers (postsecondary) are 6%.
- BLS May 2025 medians are now available. Several moved up from May 2024 (for example financial managers $166,570 versus $161,700).
- Product management pay has a very wide spread. Payscale's all-employer entry-level APM average base is about $77K. Levels.fyi shows Meta (one top-paying employer) averaging about $174K total at the entry level and about $612K at L6.
- S&P 500 CEO median pay was $17.7 million in 2025 (AP and Equilar, 337 CEOs). BLS's chief executive median is about $214K because it covers all employers, mostly small ones.
- Faculty supply claims in the first draft came from 2014 to 2020 sources. The current AACSB report and BLS both describe a limited number of positions.
- No source names a weed-out course. A McIntire (UVA) student blog calls the weed-out idea a myth. Michigan Ross students name different "hardest" courses (accounting, an intro business course, operations).

## Undergraduate (Years 1 to 4)

Degree structure:

- BBA programs run about 120 credits. Examples: UM-Dearborn (minimum 120 credits, foundation courses need a C or better), Michigan Ross (120 credits with a 54-credit non-business requirement and a 30-credit transfer cap for first-years), LIU (120 credits, six concentrations), Texas State (120 semester hours minimum).
- UM-Dearborn foundation: financial and managerial accounting, macro and microeconomics, business problem solving with computer applications, college algebra or precalculus, business statistics, writing and business writing. Upper level: project management and leadership, managerial communications, corporate responsibility, managerial economics, advanced statistics, corporate finance, information systems, strategic management.
- Marymount's BBA core covers accounting, business law, economics, finance, international business, management, management science and marketing, with a minor typically starting junior year.
- LIU's core adds entrepreneurial thinking, selling and negotiating, data analytics, and computational thinking and coding to accounting, finance, management and marketing.
- Emory Goizueta requires prerequisites before entry, a statistics course or exam, a Data and Decision Analytics course, a 10-credit co-curricular core, an Excel course before entry, and four more Tech Tools courses. It lists 70+ electives.
- Brooklyn College offers tracks including business economics, e-business, entrepreneurial management, international business, leadership and HR, and marketing. It requires at least one writing-intensive course and a capstone seminar.
- UT Austin expects freshman-year calculus (Mathematics 408Q) and a foreign language proficiency.

Weeder courses:

- Not established by any source. McIntire's student blog (2020) says "there is no weed-out class" for its application and names introductory financial accounting, management accounting and a foundations course as the ones people argue about.
- Michigan Ross student correspondents (2018) each named a different hard course: Accounting 300 (applying concepts to varied scenarios), BA 200 (an unfamiliar, theory-heavy format), and Technology and Operations 313 (applying operations concepts to real problems). These are three individual views.
- The first draft called accounting the course that filters the most students and corporate finance a "genuine weeder." Neither is supported.

Skills and tools:

- Excel is formally taught (Emory requires an Excel course before entry).
- Brookings (October 2025, carried from finance-research.md): "Excel is no longer a differentiator" and Python is becoming the new Excel in finance.
- A sponsored Poets&Quants article (April 2025, low confidence) says Python, SQL, Tableau, ChatGPT and Salesforce Einstein are becoming the norm in tech-forward roles.
- General descriptions of what courses and tools do (case-method teaching, DCF and LBO models, slide decks, ERP and CRM platforms) are general knowledge and were not source-verified in this pass.

Beyond the classroom:

- Best documented in finance recruiting (carried from finance-research.md): junior-summer internship applications open in the fall of sophomore year, and summer analyst programs convert to full-time offers at roughly 70 to 80% in strong markets (Prospect Rock). Referrals, selective clubs and alumni networks are an advantage (Adventis, Leland).
- Evidence outside finance (marketing, operations, HR, consulting) was not collected. The claim that internships matter more than GPA is not supported by any source found.
- Case competitions as a recruiting audition: not verified.

## Master's (Years about 5 to 7)

Program types (Leland, March 2026; UND, April 2025; mbaschools.org):

- Two-year full-time MBA: immersive, recruiting-heavy; suits career changers.
- One-year or accelerated MBA: listed as a distinct format.
- Part-time and online MBA: work while studying. More than 400 AACSB-accredited institutions offer an MBA partly or fully online, up from about 150 a decade ago. Some online programs cost under $30,000 in total.
- Executive MBA: typically 10 or more years of experience, cohort-based, weekend or modular.
- Specialized or dual-degree MBA (analytics, healthcare, international, MBA with law, medicine or a technical master's).
- Specialized business master's (for example MS in Finance, Analytics, Management): shorter and narrower. Forbes (via GMAC) puts the median MBA salary more than $25,000 above other business master's. National University (carried from finance-research.md) describes the MS in Finance as cheaper than an MBA on average.

Who attends:

- US News: it is common for MBA candidates to be working professionals rather than students going straight from undergrad, and work experience is often a factor in admission. Requirements vary by school. A specific "2 to 5 years" requirement was not found in any source.

Funding and cost:

- Average MBA cost about $60,410 (Education Data Initiative 2024, via Research.com). Top-ranked US programs averaged $253,179 in 2025 (BusinessBecause, via Research.com).
- US News: the 20 cheapest online MBAs charge out-of-state part-time students an average of $322 per credit hour in 2025-26.
- MBAs are largely self-funded, unlike funded research doctorates.
- Cost is not just tuition: Research.com tells readers to add fees, living costs, travel, interest and lost wages.

Outcomes:

- Forbes (December 2025): projected 2025 median MBA salary $125,000 (GMAC), about $50,000 above bachelor's holders. GMAC's 2025 Corporate Recruiters Survey: more than 90% of global employers planned to hire MBAs.
- Research.com gives a different projection ($85,000 to $120,000 median starting salary) and, in another article, an average of $165,372 for MBA holders (Nov 2025). The $165K figure has no stated method and was not used.
- Forbes's source says the MBA is clearly worth it for consulting, investment banking, private equity and tech product management; value elsewhere depends on goals.
- Application volume at leading schools rose 7% in 2025 (Research.com).
- GMAC's 2022 Corporate Recruiters Survey: technology (15%), finance (14%) and marketing (13%) had the largest shares of post-MBA job offers (Research.com).

Curriculum signals:

- Sponsored article (Poets&Quants, April 2025, low confidence): leading programs such as NYU Stern (Tech MBA), Carnegie Mellon Tepper and MIT Sloan are building AI and analytics into the curriculum, and AI strategy electives are common.
- UND describes hybrid programs using consulting projects, immersive simulations and experiential trips.
- Columbia Business School (April 2026): its goal is graduates who can lead AI implementation and use judgment, communication and change leadership.

## PhD / Research (Years about 8+)

PhD versus DBA:

- PhD: theory building and research training for academic careers. Babson says 5 to 7 years, typically full-time. USF says the best programs take four or five years. FIU says many programs pay a stipend and tuition waiver, expect no outside work, and admit a limited number of students. Miami says funding exists because PhD students support faculty research and undergraduate teaching.
- DBA: practitioner doctorate for experienced professionals. About three years, part-time or hybrid, cohort-based, usually self-paid (Miami, USF, Acacia). Graduates become executives, consultants or Professors of Practice (Babson). Rollins and Drexel describe it as applied research on real business problems.
- PhD students pick a discipline (marketing, finance, accounting, MIS and others, per USF). The DBA is interdisciplinary.
- FIU: the business PhD has existed in the US since the 1920s; the DBA began at Harvard Business School in 1953.
- Accounting faculty note that DBA graduates who teach at large universities usually do so as instructors rather than on the tenure track (TXCPA, 2020).

What doctoral work looks like (finance specifics carried from finance-research.md):

- USF: a common research-methods core runs through PhD concentrations.
- Years one and two: coursework and a qualifying or comprehensive exam; years three to five: research papers, dissertation proposal and a job-market paper (Oregon, Miami, Memphis, Ohio State, for finance).
- TXCPA (2020, accounting): papers take at least three years from start to a top-tier journal, with several revisions. Publications are the main tenure criterion. This was reported for accounting and not verified for other fields.

Research frontiers (current):

- AI and work: Academy of Management scholars' 2026 predictions (January 2026) include leaders adopting AI efficiencies (Aguinis), dangers of human-like AI at work (Tröster) and human-AI collaboration (Myers).
- AACSB Research Roundup (23 September 2026): "experimentalist work intensification" when employees build AI (Karunakaran, Kellogg, Wiesenfeld); AI adoption "widespread but shallow" (Bick, Blandin, Deming, Schumacher; NBER w35677); "Task-GenAI Fit" from interviews with 33 consultants (Tuczek and colleagues); employment effects of AI on young workers (Brynjolfsson, Chandar, Chen).
- AOM 2026 predictions also name a rethink of DEI models, the growing role of line managers, interpersonal ties, performance evaluation practices, and Indigenous perspectives on the purpose of business.
- Finance (carried from finance-research.md): climate and ESG finance, household finance, entrepreneurial finance and venture capital, FinTech, behavioral finance (Duke Fuqua), private credit (arXiv 2026), and hybrid machine-learning and econometric methods (arXiv review).

Academic job market:

- AACSB 2025-26 Staff Compensation and Demographics Survey (published 30 June 2026), Americas macroregion, 27,105 faculty at 386 institutions, all ranks: accounting mean $172,551 (median $159,293); finance including banking $190,553 ($170,000); marketing $158,283 ($145,600); CIS/MIS $148,654 ($140,350); management $141,462 ($130,000). Assistant professor averages read from a chart (rounded to the nearest $1K): accounting about $170K, finance about $180K, marketing about $147K, CIS/MIS about $142K, management about $133K.
- AACSB's own words: "The new-doctorate market remains competitive" and "Many institutions are navigating salary and hiring freezes." It gives no hiring-volume data.
- BLS (OOH, fetched 2026-11-23): business teachers, postsecondary: 6% projected growth 2025 to 2035 (about 6,100 jobs), median pay $99,080 in May 2025. All postsecondary teachers: 7%. BLS adds that "in all disciplines" there is expected to be a limited number of full-time tenure and non-tenure positions. OEWS May 2025 mean annual wage for business teachers: $114,720.
- NEA data (Poets&Quants, May 2025, carried from finance-research.md): business faculty averaged $117,000 at four-year institutions in 2023-24, third behind health professions and engineering/architecture.
- Supply claims are dated: Roger Martin (June 2020 opinion) describes a structural shortage of business PhDs, and TXCPA (2020) describes a persistent accounting-faculty shortage citing 2014 and 2015 papers. Neither is current evidence.

## Tools and AI

Standard tools:

- Excel and spreadsheets; statistics and analytics software; accounting and finance coursework tools (see Undergraduate for what is and is not sourced).

AI and hiring:

- Bloomberg (Nov 2025): MBBs expect AI proficiency; consulting is "facing a tsunami" if AI can do research and synthesis.
- HBR (Oct 2025): AI is dismantling the large-junior-class model professional-services firms used to supply a few partners.
- Tuck's Joe Hall (BusinessBecause, Jan 2026): AI is expected to hit early-career and entry-level roles, which MBA graduates will oversee.
- BCG (March 2026): 50 to 55% of US jobs will be reshaped by AI over two to three years; task automation is not the same as job loss.
- Columbia Business School (April 2026): financial analyst jobs are the main MBA-relevant area seeing early AI impact; AI augments mid-level financial analysts and managers; researchers are widening their scope and hiring more assistants.
- Stanford (Brynjolfsson, Chandar, Chen, via AACSB, 23 September 2026): employment of 22 to 25 year olds fell 11% in high-AI-exposure occupations and rose 10% in low-exposure ones (US payroll data through June 2026). The authors say the data does not prove AI caused the gap.
- PwC's 2025 Global AI Jobs Barometer, as reported by NDNU (secondary source): workers with AI skills earn 56% more than peers in the same jobs without them. Payscale (October 2025, same source): job postings requesting AI skills rose 33% between May and October 2025.
- GTM 8020 (commercial blog, low confidence): AI proficiency adds 15 to 22% to marketing salaries.
- Practitioner view on hype: AACSB's roundup describes AI adoption as widespread but shallow, and its consultant interviews find AI value depends on the task.

## Career Paths

All figures are US. BLS medians for May 2025 are computed from the median hourly wage times 2,080 hours (the BLS table has no median annual column); where BLS publishes an annual median directly, it is noted. "Industry" means a practitioner or recruiter source, not BLS. Titles are common job ladders and were not verified one by one.

### 1. Management Consulting

- Titles: Analyst or Associate, Consultant, Engagement Manager, Principal, Partner.
- Reality: roughly a 1% acceptance funnel at McKinsey, BCG and Bain (McKinsey's figure from the firm; BCG and Bain are coaching-firm estimates). AI is shrinking the junior tier (HBR) and MBB firms expect AI proficiency (Bloomberg).
- Salary: undergraduate hires at consulting firms started at about $100,000 base at several firms and about $90,000 (EY) to $132,000 (PwC Strategy&) total compensation (Management Consulted via Poets&Quants, January 2026); Bain's $140,000 covers undergraduate and master's hires combined. MBA hires at McKinsey, Bain and BCG: $200,000 base for 2027 starts, bonuses separate (Poets&Quants, 27 October 2026). Class of 2025 MBAs in consulting: median starting salary plus bonus $205,310 (Bloomberg). Partner pay was not sourced.
- Sources: Hacking the Case Interview; Poets&Quants (two articles); Bloomberg; HBR.

### 2. Corporate Finance and Investment Banking

- Titles: Financial Analyst, IB Analyst, Associate, Finance Manager or VP, CFO.
- Reality: corporate finance is broadly accessible. BLS projects financial managers to grow 10% from 2025 to 2035 (65,600 openings a year; an earlier page said 15%). Investment banking is the high-intensity lane (60 to 80 hours a week, carried from finance-research.md).
- Salary: BLS May 2025: financial managers $166,570 median; financial and investment analysts about $102,750. First-year IB analysts about $170,000 to $195,000 all-in; bulge-bracket associates $300,000 to $450,000 (Wall Street Careers and Mergers and Inquisitions, carried from finance-research.md). Managing director pay was not sourced.
- Sources: BLS OOH financial managers [2026-11-23]; BLS OEWS May 2025 [2026-11-23]; finance-research.md.

### 3. Marketing and Brand

- Titles: Coordinator, Brand or Product Marketing Manager, Marketing Manager, Director, CMO.
- Reality: BLS marketing managers: 10th percentile below $81,900, 90th above $239,200 (May 2024), so pay varies widely. BLS projects 6% growth (2024 to 2034). GTM 8020 (commercial blog) puts product marketing managers highest at a $152,553 median.
- Salary: BLS May 2025 medians: market research analysts and marketing specialists about $78,770; marketing managers about $166,800 (May 2024: $161,030). Glassdoor entry-level marketing manager average $91,747 (via GTM 8020). CMO pay was not sourced.
- Sources: BLS OOH marketing managers; BLS OEWS May 2025; GTM 8020.

### 4. Operations and Supply Chain

- Titles: Operations Analyst, Supply Chain Manager, Operations or Plant Manager, Director of Operations, COO or CSCO.
- Reality: BLS logisticians: 18% projected growth 2025 to 2035, 26,600 openings a year, bachelor's typical. A recruiter blog citing ASCM's 2025 report says directors often exceed $225,000 in total compensation. The first draft's "recession-resistant" claim has no source.
- Salary: BLS May 2025: logisticians $82,320 median; general and operations managers about $105,770 (May 2024: $102,950). Chief supply chain officer total compensation $300,000 to $500,000+ (SCOPE Recruiting, commercial).
- Sources: BLS OOH logisticians [2026-11-23]; BLS OEWS May 2025; BLS OOH top executives; SCOPE Recruiting.

### 5. Product Management

- Titles: Associate PM, Product Manager, Senior PM, Group PM or Director, VP or CPO.
- Reality: the pay spread is huge. Payscale's all-employer figure for entry-level associate product managers is $77,044 average base, $55,000 to $108,000 total compensation (677 profiles, last updated March 2025). Meta, one high-paying employer, averages about $174K total at the entry level (RPM, L3), $263K at L4, $419K at L5 and $612K at L6 (Levels.fyi, updated 22 November 2026). A sponsored article names "AI product manager" as a new hybrid role. Entry routes into PM from a generalist business degree were not researched.
- Salary: see Reality.
- Sources: Payscale [2026-11-23]; Levels.fyi Meta [2026-11-23]; Poets&Quants (sponsored).

### 6. Human Resources and People Operations

- Titles: HR Generalist, HR Business Partner, HR Manager, Director of People, CHRO.
- Reality: BLS HR managers: 5% projected growth 2024 to 2034, 17,900 openings a year, 10th percentile below $83,790, 90th above $239,200 (May 2024). Highest-paying industries in May 2024 were professional, scientific and technical services ($163,970) and management of companies ($163,180).
- Salary: BLS May 2025: HR managers about $149,280 (May 2024: $140,030); HR specialists about $75,940. Payscale: entry-level "HR manager" titles average $59,310 total compensation (243 salaries). CHRO pay was not sourced. The first draft called HR automation-resistant; no source supports that.
- Sources: BLS OOH HR managers; BLS OEWS May 2025; HR University; Payscale.

### 7. General Management and C-Suite

- Titles: Operations Manager, General Manager, VP or GM, COO or President, CEO.
- Reality: BLS top executives: 4% projected growth (2024 to 2034), about 331,000 openings a year. Forbes notes chief executives at large corporations often hold MBAs. The first draft's "15 to 25 years" has no source.
- Salary: BLS chief executives median $206,420 (May 2024), about $213,990 in May 2025; general and operations managers $102,950 (May 2024), about $105,770 in May 2025. S&P 500 median CEO pay was $17.7 million in 2025 (AP and Equilar; 337 CEOs; up nearly 6%).
- Sources: BLS OOH top executives; BLS OEWS May 2025; Scripps/AP [2026-11-23]; Forbes Advisor.

### 8. Entrepreneurship

- Titles: Founder, Co-Founder or CEO, Small-Business Owner, Franchisee.
- Reality: BLS (establishments opened March 2013, private sector): 79.6% open after 1 year, 50.6% after 5, 34.7% after 10. These are establishments, so they include branches and closures that are not failed firms. The "90% of startups fail" figure is not supported here. One sponsored article claims AI is lowering the barriers to starting a company (low confidence).
- Salary: no reliable median exists. Founder pay was not sourced.
- Sources: BLS TED [2026-11-23]; Poets&Quants (sponsored).

## Uncertainties and Regional Variation

- Everything is US-centric. The one non-US source found (Collegedunia, India) describes a three-year, six-semester BBA, which differs from the US four-year BBA. Other regions were not researched.
- BLS wage figures: May 2025 annual medians are computed from median hourly wages times 2,080 (financial managers and logisticians matched BLS's published annual figures to within $10). May 2024 figures are labeled as such. BLS projections have moved to 2025 to 2035 on updated pages; pages not re-fetched may still show 2024 to 2034 growth rates.
- AACSB figures cover participating institutions in the Americas macroregion, not all US schools. Assistant professor values are read from a chart and rounded. Contract length (nine versus twelve months) was not verified.
- Faculty supply: shortage claims are from 2014 to 2020. Current sources (AACSB 2026, BLS) describe a competitive market with a limited number of positions. The first draft's "strong placement" framing is not supported.
- Low-confidence or commercial sources: Hacking the Case Interview (coaching firm; BCG and Bain figures are estimates), GTM 8020, SCOPE Recruiting, Research.com ($165,372 average), Payscale (self-reported), NDNU (secondary report of PwC), Poets&Quants sponsored article, MBA & Beyond.
- The MBA median starting salary differs across sources ($125,000 versus $85,000 to $120,000).
- Meta is one employer and not representative of product management pay.
- Claims removed or softened from the first draft because no source supports them: accounting and corporate finance as "weeders"; "Excel is tested directly by recruiters"; internships mattering "more than GPA"; case competitions as a recruiting audition; "every top program runs AI electives as core, not optional"; the "2 to 5 years" MBA requirement; "top PhD programs admit a tiny handful"; "getting in is harder than getting placed"; "platform and gig-economy strategy" as a frontier; "the 15 to 22% AI pay premium is documented"; "MBB admit rates in the low single digits"; "$150K to $200K IB analyst"; "Partner $1M+"; "MD $500K to $1M+"; "APM $110K to $140K"; "PM total comp $150K to $250K+"; early-career salary bands for coordinators, analysts and generalists; "recession-resistant" operations; HR as "automation-resistant"; "roughly 90% of startups fail"; "public-company CEO $1M to $20M+".
- General-knowledge descriptions kept without sources: what each core course teaches, case-method teaching, DCF and LBO models, slide decks, ERP and CRM platforms, and title ladders.
- The executive DBA at Drexel is open to domestic students only (its page); other DBA eligibility rules were not checked.
