# Art & Design: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Graphic designers** — stable career ID `graphic-designers`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `27-1024`.

Graphic design only; UX, industrial design, fine art and art direction have other task mixes.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 27-1024.00 Graphic Designers.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 253,100 jobs in 2025; -1.7% projected change, 2025–35; 16,000 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 27-1024](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

Image and layout generation competes directly with commodity production while making exploration faster. BLS explicitly incorporates productivity and substitution in graphic design projections; the online freelance study finds reduced work and earnings for exposed categories and image-tool releases. US copyrightability depends on human expressive contribution, not prompting alone. Client goals, accessibility and final production still matter.

**Independent sources:** [Factors affecting occupational utilization](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/factors-affecting-occupational-utilization.htm), [Copyright and AI, Part 2: Copyrightability](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf), [Short-Term Effects of Generative AI on Employment](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4544582).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

2029–31 agents could produce campaign variants at scale; low-budget work may move in-house and agencies may compress junior production hours. More affordable design may expand demand, especially for custom products, but whether the volume offsets labor savings is unknown. Rights/provenance and brand consistency will affect adoption.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Build a portfolio with sketches, briefs, user feedback and revision history.
- Learn typography, accessible layout and production specs without generation.
- Keep asset licenses and provenance records and practice presenting design decisions..
