# Physics: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Physicists** — stable career ID `physicists`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `19-2012`.

Physicists, not astronomy, all engineering, all physics faculty, or all physics graduates.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 19-2012.00 Physicists.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 23,200 jobs in 2025; +7.2% projected change, 2025–35; 1,300 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 19-2012](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

AIP follow-up survey reports AI use or development among US physics degree recipients across jobs. Scientific computation and specialized ML can classify or model data, while GenAI can aid code and text; vendor research examples are selected and expert-led. Experimental design, instruments, calibration and physical interpretation still matter.

**Independent sources:** [AI Use Among Physics Degree Recipients](https://www.aip.org/statistics/percentage-of-new-physics-bachelors-masters-and-phds-who-use-or-develop-ai-degree-recipients-from-academic-year-2023-24), [Accelerating Mathematical and Scientific Discovery with Gemini Deep Think](https://deepmind.google/blog/accelerating-mathematical-and-scientific-discovery-with-gemini-deep-think/), [Alerts and brokers](https://rubinobservatory.org/for-scientists/data-products/alerts-and-brokers).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents may accelerate calculations, simulations and manuscripts. Reduced computation cost may permit new research and engineering work, but labs require physical facilities, verified measurements, safety reviews and funding. Students must still understand units, assumptions, uncertainty and experimental context.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Build a reproducible simulation checked against physical limits.
- Seek supervised instrumentation and measurement practice.
- Learn uncertainty analysis and maintain transparent code and evidence records..
