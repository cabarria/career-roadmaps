# Molecular Biology: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Biochemists and biophysicists** — stable career ID `biochemists-and-biophysicists`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `19-1021`.

Biochemists and biophysicists include non-molecular specialists and exclude some molecular biology jobs.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 19-1021.00 Biochemists and Biophysicists.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 35,200 jobs in 2025; +12.3% projected change, 2025–35; 2,900 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 19-1021](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

Global Wiley research respondents report growing AI use; it is not a molecular-biologist sample. Generative writing and analysis assistance can support routine research documentation and literature workflows. Human researchers remain accountable for evidence quality, interpretation, research integrity and appropriate oversight. NIH requires disclosure and confidentiality in relevant work; scientific prediction tools are distinct from language assistants.

**Independent sources:** [ExplanAItions 2025–2026: Evolution of AI in Research](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/about-us/ai/study), [Guidelines for the Conduct of Research, ninth edition](https://oir.nih.gov/system/files/media/file/2026-09/guidelines-conduct_research.pdf).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

More integrated data and literature workflows may shift scientists from manual processing to verification, stewardship and interpretation. Research funding, evidence generation, safety/ethics review and institutional constraints determine adoption and hiring. Academic and industry pathways have different incentives.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn experimental reasoning, statistics and research ethics in supervised courses.
- Produce a reproducible public-data analysis and document validation.
- Seek supervised research training focused on evidence quality and appropriate data governance..
