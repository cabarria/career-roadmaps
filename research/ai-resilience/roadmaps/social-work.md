# Social Work: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Social workers** — stable career ID `social-workers`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `21-1020`.

BLS umbrella for child/family/school, medical, mental health/substance abuse and all other; residual lacks detailed tasks.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 21-1021.00 Child, Family, and School Social Workers; 21-1022.00 Healthcare Social Workers; 21-1023.00 Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Workers; 21-1029.00 Social Workers, All Other.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 816,100 jobs in 2025; +6.2% projected change, 2025–35; 69,100 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 21-1020](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

NASW and UT Austin national survey reports that most responding practitioners use AI; NASW clinical standards emphasize transparency, confidentiality and judgment. Record drafts, resource lookup and forms are plausible aids, but safeguarding, crisis response, therapeutic care and advocacy depend on personal context and statutory responsibilities. The survey collected 1,179 responses from October 2025 to February 2026.

**Independent sources:** [Practice Standards for Clinical Social Workers](https://www.socialworkers.org/Practice/NASW-Practice-Standards-Guidelines/NASW-Practice-Standards-for-Clinical-Social-Workers), [National Survey Finds Most Social Workers Already Using AI](https://www.socialworkers.org/News/News-Releases/ID/3437/National-Survey-Finds-Most-Social-Workers-Already-Using-Artificial-Intelligence-Calling-For-Ethical-Guidance-and-Professional-Leadership), [The AI Jobs Transition Framework](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/the-ai-jobs-transition-framework_report.pdf).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Approved case-management agents might reduce paperwork and connect clients to resources; resource shortages, social-service funding and risk of biased eligibility decisions constrain adoption. Administrative staffing may shift separately from home visits, care coordination and clinical work.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Practice supervised case assessment and client advocacy.
- Learn confidentiality, informed consent, documentation and mandated-reporting rules.
- Evaluate resource recommendations for access and accuracy without exposing case data..
