## Current evidence

AI images and drafting aids can accelerate early options and document preparation. AIA's 2025 individual survey found only 6% regular use, whereas its 2026 firm survey found 74% of firms used AI in 2025 operations; these are different denominators, questions and samples. NCARB's position keeps responsibility with the architect. Distinguish visual concept generation from precise coordinated drawings and established BIM automation.

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

By 2029–31, integrated agents could generate drawing variants and check submittals inside approved BIM systems. Firms may need fewer production hours per commission, but better cost control might generate more design services. Smaller firms will face integration costs; proprietary files and errors require review. The junior route could lose repetitive redlining through which architects learned details.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Practice drawing and detailing from first principles before comparing generated versions.
- Document a BIM project with code checks and field observations.
- Plan supervised AXP experience across categories, not just automated production..
