Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Mechanical engineers** — stable career ID `mechanical-engineers`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `17-2141`.

General mechanical engineers; automotive and fuel-cell O*NET specialties are not separately weighted.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 17-2141.00 Mechanical Engineers; 17-2141.01 Fuel Cell Engineers; 17-2141.02 Automotive Engineers.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 298,500 jobs in 2025; +11.2% projected change, 2025–35; 17,800 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 17-2141](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

CAD, simulation and coding assistance can accelerate iterations; generative topology optimization, conventional automation and industrial robotics are separate technologies. NCEES emphasizes that professional responsibility and verification stay with licensed engineers. Software survey evidence is only adjacent, not mechanical-engineer adoption. ASME and Articul8 announced a domain-specific standards model in July 2026, evidence of investment in retrieval support, not validated design performance or sector adoption.

**Independent sources:** [Policy Manual, PS 6.10 Responsible Use of AI in Engineering and Surveying](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Policy-manual_August-2025_web.pdf), [The AI Jobs Transition Framework](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/the-ai-jobs-transition-framework_report.pdf), [2026 Developer Survey: AI](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai), [Domain-Specific GenAI Model for Engineering Standards](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/media-inquiries/press-releases/articul8-ai-and-asme-announce-industry-first-domain-specific-genai-model-for-engineering-standards).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agentic CAD and review may cut routine documentation and design iterations. New product variants and manufacturing investment may increase testing and integration demand, while some junior drafting hours disappear. Hardware failures, qualification, safety and supplier constraints will remain review gates.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Prototype and measure performance against a simulation.
- Learn CAD, GD&T, materials, failure analysis and verification without agents.
- Seek shop, manufacturing or field experience and document design tradeoffs..
