# Pharmacy: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Pharmacists** — stable career ID `pharmacists`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `29-1051`.

Pharmacists as occupation; not all industry/regulatory or informatics positions held by PharmD graduates.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 29-1051.00 Pharmacists.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 325,200 jobs in 2025; +5.2% projected change, 2025–35; 12,500 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 29-1051](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

ASHP's statement calls for pharmacy-led selection, validation and monitoring of AI and distinguishes tasks suitable for proven algorithmic automation. Traditional dispensing robotics, barcode verification and decision-support rules already affect pipelines separately. Generative tools can draft medication information but require expert check for safety and current evidence.

**Independent sources:** [Statement on Artificial Intelligence in Pharmacy](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/policy-guidelines/docs/statements/artificial-intelligence-in-pharmacy.pdf), [Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices), [2026 Physician Survey on Augmented Intelligence](https://www.ama-assn.org/sites/ama-assn.org/files/2026-03/physician-ai-sentiment-report_0.pdf).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Community dispensing could centralize further; hospital and ambulatory clinical roles may gain time for patient review. Agentic coverage/document workflows could alter managed care staffing. Payment for clinical services, state rules, validated systems, oversight and patient counseling shape demand.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn medication review and counseling from reliable primary information.
- Practice checking documentation and interactions in approved scenarios.
- Seek hospital, community or informatics rotations with quality-improvement projects..
