Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Registered nurses** — stable career ID `registered-nurses`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `29-1141`.

RNs with named O*NET specialty examples; NP, CRNA and midwife are different Matrix codes, while some other advanced roles are RN subtypes.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 29-1141.00 Registered Nurses; 29-1141.01 Acute Care Nurses; 29-1141.02 Advanced Practice Psychiatric Nurses; 29-1141.03 Critical Care Nurses; 29-1141.04 Clinical Nurse Specialists.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 3,465,400 jobs in 2025; +5.6% projected change, 2025–35; 180,800 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 29-1141](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

ANA ethical guidance explicitly preserves nursing assessment and responsibility. Clinician scribe evidence is adjacent and cannot be treated as measured RN savings. Chart drafting, workflow and educational materials can be assisted; bedside examination, medications, coordination and escalation require professional judgment. Existing monitoring and staffing algorithms are not all GenAI. A November 2026 US inpatient nursing ambient-documentation randomized trial protocol measures EHR audit-log time per shift hour; results are not yet reported. It is a concrete research route to close the direct evidence gap.

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents could prepare documentation and handoff drafts, possibly freeing bedside time, but employers could use productivity to raise caseloads. Informatics and administrative RNs have different task mix. Staffing rules, patient acuity, union agreements, privacy and accountability constrain changes; advanced practice is distinct.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Practice bedside assessments, handoffs and clinical reasoning without AI.
- Train on safe documentation and error detection in approved simulations.
- Seek placements spanning settings and learn scope-of-practice and escalation..
