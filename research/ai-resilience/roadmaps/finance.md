## Current evidence

FINRA describes GenAI financial-data synthesis and document applications while existing supervisory rules remain in force for member firms. Consulting and vendor task studies support plausible research acceleration, not a banking or analyst displacement rate. Model outputs require reconciliation to filings, current data and risk policy. FINRA's December 2025 oversight report documents member-firm uses including information extraction, reports, workflow automation and financial modeling, without providing an analyst adoption denominator.

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents may generate pitch books, research screens and models; banks/funds could change analyst staffing and apprenticeship or expand coverage. Access to proprietary data, compliance, audit trails and client trust constrain adoption. Wealth management may see expansion if personalized service becomes cheaper; quant workflows have different risk controls.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Build valuation and accounting models manually, then audit AI outputs against filings.
- Study risk, statistics and governance, especially data leakage and assumptions.
- Seek client, deal or investment research projects with documented diligence..
