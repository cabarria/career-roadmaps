# Biomedical Engineering: AI resilience

Evidence cutoff **2026-11-25** · United States · scenario horizon **2029–31**.

## Featured occupation

**Bioengineers and biomedical engineers** — stable career ID `biomedical-engineers`; BLS 2025 National Employment Matrix `17-2031`.

Biomedical engineers, not clinical physicians or all life-science researchers.

**O*NET-SOC 2019 matches (release 31.0):** 17-2031.00 Bioengineers and Biomedical Engineers.

**Labor context (not AI's effect):** 23,800 jobs in 2025; +7.6% projected change, 2025–35; 1,200 average annual openings ([BLS Table 1.2, row 17-2031](https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm)).

## Current evidence

FDA's September 2026 inventory exceeds 1,600 AI-enabled medical device authorizations, mostly a mix of AI types rather than proof of GenAI replacing engineers. Document drafting and software support are plausible aids; an engineer must validate performance against intended use and clinical evidence, maintain quality records and evaluate risk. A device authorization is not evidence of actual clinical adoption.

**Independent sources:** [Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices), [Policy Manual, PS 6.10 Responsible Use of AI in Engineering and Surveying](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Policy-manual_August-2025_web.pdf), [Guidelines for the Conduct of Research, ninth edition](https://oir.nih.gov/system/files/media/file/2026-09/guidelines-conduct_research.pdf).

## 2029–31 conditional scenario

Agents could accelerate design review and documentation in validated workflows, with human review gates. Additional device variants and validation work may expand demand, while repetitive analysis and reporting become cheaper. Device software changes, safety, privacy and regulatory submissions make adoption uneven.

This scenario assumes approved data access, higher agent reliability on bounded workflows, and human review appropriate to risk. A slower rollout preserves more manual work; faster adoption could compress routine junior tasks. Neither establishes employment probabilities.

## Student preparation

- Learn engineering fundamentals, statistics and quality systems.
- Complete a supervised device or instrumentation project with requirements and verification records.
- Practice documenting assumptions, data rights and model limitations..
